PhotoSort v4.0 Mathematical Architecture

Designed to overcome classic single-scale Laplacian failures where high-contrast blurry edges score high and shallow depth-of-field portraits score low.

1. Multi-Band Frequency Acutance (DoG Ratio)

Computes fine high-frequency detail |G(σ=0.8) - G(σ=1.8)| divided by coarse ramp energy |G(σ=1.8) - G(σ=4.2)| along the 90th-percentile structural edges. True optical focus produces narrow impulse transitions (Ratio > 0.48), whereas out-of-focus high-contrast edges have wide ramps (Ratio < 0.30) and are penalized immediately.

2. 16-Zone (4x4) Spatial Grid & Bokeh Isolation

Partitions the canonical 1024px frame into 16 spatial cells. By comparing the top-4 subject zones against the 6 smoothest background zones, the engine detects intentional Portrait Bokeh (Bokeh Ratio ≥ 2.6x) and rewards subject sharpness instead of averaging in blurred backgrounds.

3. Subject-Aware Exposure & Dynamic Range

Measures luminance and clipping separately inside the central/subject ROI vs. the frame perimeter. High-key white studio backdrops and low-key dark night backdrops are no longer falsely penalized when the subject is well-exposed.

4. Directional Motion-Blur Anisotropy

Evaluates orthogonal Sobel gradient ratios alongside edge width to distinguish directional camera shake from spherical lens defocus.
